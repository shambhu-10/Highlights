# Highlights — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

Highlights saves text you choose to highlight on web pages, so you can find it again.

## What is stored
For each highlight: the page URL and title, the highlighted text with a few words around it, your note, and timestamps. Nothing is collected from pages you don't highlight, and browsing history is never recorded.

## Where it is stored
- **In your browser**, always.
- **In your Highlights account**, only if you sign in. It is stored in a Supabase database. Row-level security means only your account can read or change your highlights.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address to identify your account. We never receive your Google password.

## What we don't do
No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of data with anyone. Data is used only to show and sync your own highlights.

## Your control
Export everything as Markdown or JSON from the library at any time. Delete a highlight and it is deleted from every synced device. To delete your account and all of its data, email shambhu04.kumar@gmail.com.
